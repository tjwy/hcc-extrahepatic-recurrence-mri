# Model weights

Trained checkpoints are not included because they were derived from restricted human-participant imaging data and require institutional review before redistribution.

Qualified researchers may request access from the corresponding author identified in the published article. Requests are subject to ethics approval, a data-use agreement, institutional privacy and security review, and confirmation that the intended use is consistent with participant protections and third-party licence terms.

Code execution does not require the manuscript checkpoints when models are trained from scratch. ImageNet and Med3D initialization weights remain subject to their original distribution terms.
